Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding joining Campus Collective and participating in our events.

Who can join Campus Collective?

Any registered collegiate student across any department (Engineering, Design, Business, Sciences, Humanities) and any year of study can apply.

Do I need prior experience to join?

Passion, ambition, and consistency matter far more than an existing resume. We provide internal domain mentorship to help members level up rapidly in their chosen guild.

Are workshops and hackathons free?

Yes! The majority of workshops and hackathons are fully free of cost for campus students, supported by corporate sponsors and community partners.

Can I participate in multiple domains?

Absolutely. While you will have a primary domain for operational assignments, our collective encourages cross-pollination across technical, creative, and organizational guilds.
