Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding joining Spectrum and participating in our events.

Who can join Spectrum?

Any registered student at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Ramapuram Campus across any department (Engineering, Science & Humanities, Management) and any year of study can apply.

Do I need prior experience to join?

Passion, ambition, and consistency matter far more than an existing resume. We provide internal domain mentorship to help members level up rapidly in their chosen guild.

Are Spectrum hackathons and workshops free?

Yes! The majority of Spectrum workshops and hackathons are fully free of cost for SRM Ramapuram students, supported by our corporate sponsors and institutional partners.

Can I participate in multiple domains?

Absolutely. While you will have a primary domain for operational assignments, Spectrum encourages cross-pollination across technical, creative, and organizational guilds.
